- Solve problems involving motion under gravity
- Solve problems involving multiple stages of motion
- Use the constant acceleration formulas together with velocity-time graphs

=== mistakes ===
- Using a suvat equation when the acceleration is not constant.
- Taking g as positive when upward has been chosen as the positive direction.
- Applying one equation across two stages of motion with different accelerations.
- Forgetting that at maximum height the velocity is zero but the acceleration is still g.
