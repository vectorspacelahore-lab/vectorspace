=== intro ===
The three quantities that describe motion, and the distinction between the scalar and vector versions of each. Getting this straight early saves a great deal of confusion later.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the difference between distance and displacement, and speed and velocity
- Interpret displacement-time and velocity-time graphs
- Use the gradient of a displacement-time graph as velocity
- Use the gradient and area under a velocity-time graph as acceleration and displacement

=== mistakes ===
- Treating distance and displacement as the same thing when the object reverses direction.
- Reading the area under a displacement-time graph as though it meant something.
- Ignoring the sign of velocity, which carries the direction of travel.
- Assuming a straight line on a velocity-time graph means constant velocity rather than constant acceleration.
