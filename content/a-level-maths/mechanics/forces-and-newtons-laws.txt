=== intro ===
Forces as vectors, resolved into components, then combined through F = ma. Almost every mechanics question begins with a correct force diagram.


=== syllabus ===
- Identify the forces acting on a body and represent them in a diagram
- Resolve forces into components and find the resultant of several forces
- Apply Newton's three laws of motion to a particle moving in a straight line
- Solve problems involving connected particles, including pulleys and strings

=== mistakes ===
- Omitting a force from the diagram, usually the normal contact force or the tension.
- Resolving with sine and cosine interchanged. Check which side of the angle each component sits on.
- Assuming the tension differs on either side of a smooth pulley.
- Applying F = ma with the mass of one particle when the whole system is accelerating together.
