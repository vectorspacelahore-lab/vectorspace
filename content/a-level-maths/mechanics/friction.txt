=== intro ===
The force that resists sliding, capped at μR. The subtlety is that friction is only at its maximum when motion is happening or about to happen.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the concept of limiting friction and limiting equilibrium
- Use the relationship F ≤ μR and recognise when F = μR applies
- Solve problems involving friction on horizontal and inclined planes
- Determine whether a body remains in equilibrium or begins to move

=== mistakes ===
- Setting F = μR when the body is in equilibrium but not on the point of moving.
- Using the weight instead of the normal reaction on an inclined plane. R is mg cos θ, not mg.
- Getting the direction of friction wrong — it always opposes the motion or the tendency to move.
- Forgetting that μ has no units and never appears with g attached.
