=== intro ===
Mass times velocity, and the fact that it is conserved in a collision. That conservation is what makes collision problems solvable without knowing anything about the forces involved.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand and use the concept of linear momentum
- Use the principle of conservation of linear momentum for direct impacts
- Solve problems involving collisions of two particles moving in the same straight line
- Understand the relationship between impulse and change in momentum

=== mistakes ===
- Ignoring the direction of momentum. Assign a positive direction and keep to it throughout.
- Assuming kinetic energy is conserved in a collision. Momentum is; energy usually isn't.
- Losing the sign when a particle rebounds.
- Confusing impulse with force — impulse is force times the time it acts.
