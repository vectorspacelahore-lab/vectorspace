=== intro ===
When acceleration changes, suvat fails and calculus takes over. Differentiate to go from displacement to velocity to acceleration, integrate to come back.


=== syllabus ===
- Use differentiation and integration with respect to time to solve problems with variable acceleration
- Relate displacement, velocity and acceleration by calculus
- Find the constant of integration using given initial conditions
- Find maximum and minimum values of velocity and displacement

=== mistakes ===
- Reaching for a suvat equation when acceleration is a function of time.
- Losing the constant of integration and with it the initial condition.
- Confusing the point where velocity is maximum with the point where displacement is maximum.
- Finding distance travelled by integrating without splitting at the instants when velocity is zero.
