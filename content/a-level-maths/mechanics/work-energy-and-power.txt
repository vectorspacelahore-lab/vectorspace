=== intro ===
An alternative to force-and-acceleration that ignores the detail of the journey. If a question gives speeds at two points and no time, this is usually the faster route.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand and use the concept of work done by a force
- Understand and use kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy
- Use the work-energy principle and the principle of conservation of energy
- Understand and use the relationship between power, work and time

=== mistakes ===
- Forgetting that work done by a force at an angle uses the component along the displacement.
- Omitting the work done against friction when applying conservation of energy.
- Using ½mv rather than ½mv² for kinetic energy.
- Confusing power with force — power is force times velocity, not force alone.
