=== intro ===
Two ways to use these. **Topical** papers group every past question on a single topic together — the right tool while you're still learning something. **Whole papers by session** are full exams under timed conditions, which is what you want in the last few weeks.

Each session row has the question paper, the official mark scheme, and my own solved paper showing the working rather than just the answers.
