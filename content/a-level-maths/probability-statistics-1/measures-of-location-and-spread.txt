=== intro ===
Averages and how spread out the data is around them. The mean and standard deviation are the pair you will use most, and both are sensitive to outliers in ways the median and quartiles are not.


=== syllabus ===
- Calculate and use the mean, median, mode and quartiles
- Calculate and use the range, interquartile range, variance and standard deviation
- Use the formulas for variance and standard deviation from raw data and from grouped data
- Use coded data to calculate the mean and standard deviation of the original data
- Compare distributions using measures of location and spread

=== mistakes ===
- Dividing by n − 1 where the syllabus uses n.
- Using class midpoints for grouped data but forgetting the frequencies when summing.
- Forgetting that adding a constant to coded data changes the mean but not the standard deviation.
- Confusing variance with standard deviation — one is the square of the other.
