=== intro ===
Counting without listing. The only question that matters is whether the order of selection changes the outcome.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the terms permutation and combination and solve simple problems involving selections
- Solve problems about arrangements of objects in a line, including those involving repetition and restriction
- Evaluate and use nPr and nCr
- Use permutations and combinations in probability calculations

=== mistakes ===
- Using a permutation where the order of selection is irrelevant.
- Not dividing by the factorials of repeated items when arranging objects that are alike.
- Handling 'at least one' by adding cases when counting the complement is simpler and safer.
- Treating a 'must sit together' restriction without first blocking the group as a single item.
