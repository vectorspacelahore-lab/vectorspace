=== intro ===
Measuring how likely something is, and the two rules for combining events. Most errors come from adding when you should multiply, or forgetting that events overlap.


=== syllabus ===
- Evaluate probabilities by means of enumeration of equiprobable outcomes or by calculation
- Use addition and multiplication of probabilities, understanding when each applies
- Understand the meaning of exclusive and independent events
- Calculate and use conditional probabilities

=== mistakes ===
- Adding probabilities of events that are not mutually exclusive without subtracting the overlap.
- Multiplying probabilities of events that are not independent.
- Confusing P(A|B) with P(B|A).
- Using without-replacement probabilities as though the denominators stayed the same.
