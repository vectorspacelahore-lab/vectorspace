=== intro ===
Turning a list of numbers into a picture that shows its shape. Different displays answer different questions, and part of the skill is choosing the right one.


=== syllabus ===
- Select a suitable way of presenting raw statistical data and discuss advantages and disadvantages
- Draw and interpret stem-and-leaf diagrams, box-and-whisker plots, histograms and cumulative frequency graphs
- Understand and use frequency density in histograms with unequal class widths
- Use a cumulative frequency graph to estimate the median and quartiles

=== mistakes ===
- Plotting frequency rather than frequency density on a histogram with unequal classes.
- Plotting cumulative frequency at the class midpoint instead of the upper class boundary.
- Forgetting the key on a stem-and-leaf diagram.
- Reading the median from a cumulative frequency graph at the halfway point of the axis rather than at n/2.
