=== intro ===
The bell curve that describes a great deal of natural variation. Every calculation reduces to standardising to z and reading a table.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the shape and properties of the normal distribution
- Standardise a normal variable and use the standard normal distribution tables
- Solve problems involving probabilities for a normal variable
- Find unknown means or standard deviations from given probabilities
- Use the normal distribution as an approximation to the binomial distribution, with a continuity correction

=== mistakes ===
- Forgetting the continuity correction when approximating a binomial with a normal.
- Looking up a negative z directly rather than using symmetry.
- Subtracting from 1 when the tables already give the required tail, or failing to when they don't. Sketch the curve.
- Using the variance in place of the standard deviation when standardising.
