=== intro ===
When a variable can take any value in a range, the table becomes a function and the sum becomes an integral. Everything else is the same idea.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the concept of a probability density function and use it to find probabilities
- Use the fact that the total area under a probability density function is 1
- Calculate the mean and variance of a continuous random variable
- Find and use the cumulative distribution function, and use it to find the median and other percentiles

=== mistakes ===
- Integrating over the wrong limits, or over the whole real line rather than where the function is defined.
- Forgetting that the total area must equal 1, which is usually how an unknown constant is found.
- Using E(X²) as the variance without subtracting [E(X)]².
- Differentiating and integrating the wrong way between the density function and the cumulative function.
