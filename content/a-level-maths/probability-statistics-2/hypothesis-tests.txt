=== intro ===
A formal procedure for deciding whether evidence is strong enough to abandon an assumption. The conclusion must always be stated in the context of the question.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the terms null and alternative hypothesis, significance level, rejection region and test statistic
- Formulate hypotheses and carry out a hypothesis test for a population mean
- Carry out a hypothesis test using the binomial and Poisson distributions
- Understand Type I and Type II errors and calculate their probabilities

=== mistakes ===
- Stating the conclusion as 'accept H₀'. You either reject it or you fail to reject it.
- Using a one-tailed critical value for a two-tailed test, or halving the significance level when you shouldn't.
- Not stating the conclusion in context — the marks are for the sentence about the situation, not the inequality.
- Confusing a Type I error with a Type II error.
