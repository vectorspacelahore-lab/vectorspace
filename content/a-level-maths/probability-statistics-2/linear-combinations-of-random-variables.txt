=== intro ===
What happens to the mean and variance when random variables are added, scaled or combined. The rule for variance is the one people get wrong.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the results for E(aX + b) and Var(aX + b)
- Use the results for E(aX + bY) and Var(aX + bY) for independent X and Y
- Use these results with normal, Poisson and binomial variables
- Solve problems involving the sum and difference of independent random variables

=== mistakes ===
- Writing Var(aX + b) as a Var(X) + b. The constant disappears and the multiplier is squared.
- Subtracting variances for X − Y. Variances add, whichever way the means combine.
- Applying these results to variables that are not independent.
- Confusing the sum of n observations with n times a single observation — they have different variances.
