=== intro ===
What a sample can tell you about the population it came from, and how confident you are entitled to be about it.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the distinction between a sample and a population, and the need for randomness
- Understand that the sample mean has a distribution, and use the central limit theorem
- Calculate unbiased estimates of the population mean and variance from a sample
- Determine and interpret a confidence interval for a population mean

=== mistakes ===
- Dividing by n rather than n − 1 when estimating the population variance from a sample.
- Using the population standard deviation in place of the standard error of the mean.
- Applying the central limit theorem to a small sample from a non-normal population.
- Interpreting a 95% confidence interval as a 95% probability that the true mean lies inside it.
