=== intro ===
A model for rare events occurring at random over an interval. One parameter does all the work, and both the mean and the variance equal it.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the Poisson distribution as a model and calculate probabilities
- Use the fact that the mean and variance of a Poisson distribution are both equal to λ
- Use the Poisson distribution as an approximation to the binomial where appropriate
- Use the normal distribution as an approximation to the Poisson where appropriate

=== mistakes ===
- Not scaling λ to match the interval in the question.
- Using a Poisson approximation when n is small or p is not small.
- Forgetting the continuity correction in a normal approximation to the Poisson.
- Adding the parameters of two Poisson variables when they are not independent.
