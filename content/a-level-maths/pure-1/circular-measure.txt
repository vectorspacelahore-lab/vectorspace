=== intro ===
Radians reduce arc length and sector area to s = rθ and A = ½r²θ. The work is almost always in decomposing the shaded region, not in the formulas.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the definition of a radian and convert between radians and degrees
- Use the formula s = rθ for the length of an arc
- Use the formula A = ½r²θ for the area of a sector
- Solve problems involving arcs, sectors, segments and composite regions

=== mistakes ===
- Leaving the calculator in degree mode.
- Omitting the two radii when finding the perimeter of a sector.
- Using ½ab sin C with the angle in degrees while the rest of the question is in radians.
- Rounding θ early and carrying the error into later parts.
