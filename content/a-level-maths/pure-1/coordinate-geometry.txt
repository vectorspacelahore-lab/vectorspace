=== intro ===
Lines and circles on a grid, plus one genuinely powerful technique: turning a non-linear relationship into a straight line so its constants can be read off a graph.


=== syllabus ===
- Find the equation of a straight line given sufficient information
- Interpret and use relationships between gradients of parallel and perpendicular lines
- Understand and use the equation of a circle in both centre-radius and expanded form
- Solve problems involving the intersection of lines and circles
- Transform given relationships to straight-line form

=== mistakes ===
- Using m₁m₂ = 1 rather than −1 for perpendicular lines.
- Completing the square incorrectly when converting a circle to centre-radius form, especially the signs of the centre.
- Forgetting that a tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
- In straight-line-form questions, confusing which constant is the gradient and which the intercept.
