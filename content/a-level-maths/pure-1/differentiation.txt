=== intro ===
The rate at which a function changes. From it come gradients, tangents, normals, stationary points and every optimisation question in the paper.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the gradient of a curve as a limit and use the notations f′(x) and dy/dx
- Differentiate xⁿ and simple sums, differences and constant multiples
- Apply differentiation to gradients, tangents, normals and stationary points
- Determine the nature of stationary points
- Use differentiation with connected rates of change and small increments

=== mistakes ===
- Not rewriting roots and fractions as powers of x before differentiating.
- Finding a stationary point and not classifying it when the question asks which kind it is.
- Using the tangent gradient for the normal instead of its negative reciprocal.
- Setting up connected rates of change with the chain of derivatives inverted.
