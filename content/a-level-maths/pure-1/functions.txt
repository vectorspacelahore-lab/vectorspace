=== intro ===
A function is a rule plus a domain, and the domain is where most marks are won or lost. Composition, inverses and transformations all depend on being precise about what is allowed in.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the terms function, domain, range, one-one, inverse and composition
- Identify the range of a given function and find the composition of two functions
- Determine whether a function is one-one and find the inverse of a one-one function
- Illustrate the relation between a one-one function and its inverse using graphs
- Understand and use transformations of graphs: translations, stretches and reflections

=== mistakes ===
- Applying fg and gf the wrong way round. The function nearest x acts first.
- Finding an inverse without first restricting the domain to make the function one-one.
- Combining two transformations in the wrong order — a stretch then a translation is not the same as the reverse.
- Stating the range of an inverse as the range of the original rather than its domain.
