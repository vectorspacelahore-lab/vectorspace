=== intro ===
Everything a quadratic can do is visible in its completed-square form. Vertex, range, symmetry and the number of roots all fall out of a(x + p)² + q without a sketch.


=== syllabus ===
- Carry out the process of completing the square and use it to locate the vertex and sketch the graph
- Find the discriminant and use it to determine the number of real roots
- Solve quadratic equations and quadratic inequalities in one unknown
- Solve simultaneous equations where one is linear and one is quadratic
- Recognise and solve equations that are quadratic in some function of x

=== mistakes ===
- Not factoring out the coefficient of x² before completing the square.
- Reading the vertex of a(x + p)² + q as (p, q) rather than (−p, q).
- Writing a quadratic inequality's answer as a single interval when the solution is two separate regions.
- Solving a disguised quadratic for the substitution and forgetting to convert back to x.
