=== intro ===
Binomial expansions, arithmetic progressions and geometric progressions, including the result that an infinite geometric sum can be finite.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the expansion of (a + b)ⁿ where n is a positive integer
- Recognise arithmetic and geometric progressions and use their nth-term formulas
- Use the formulas for the sum of the first n terms of arithmetic and geometric progressions
- Use the condition for convergence of a geometric progression and find the sum to infinity

=== mistakes ===
- Forgetting that in a binomial expansion the two powers must sum to n.
- Dropping the sign or the coefficient when a bracket contains a negative or a multiple of x.
- Applying the sum to infinity when |r| ≥ 1.
- Confusing the nth term with the sum to n terms.
