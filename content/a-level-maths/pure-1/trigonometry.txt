=== intro ===
Three graphs, two identities, and the persistent problem of finding every solution in an interval rather than just the one the calculator returns.


=== syllabus ===
- Sketch and use the graphs of sine, cosine and tangent, including transformations
- Use the exact values of the trigonometric functions of 30°, 45° and 60°
- Use the notations sin⁻¹x, cos⁻¹x, tan⁻¹x and the definitions of secant, cosecant and cotangent
- Use the identities tan θ = sin θ / cos θ and sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
- Find all solutions of simple trigonometrical equations in a given interval

=== mistakes ===
- Giving one solution when the interval contains several. Sketch the graph.
- Cancelling a factor of cos x and losing an entire set of solutions.
- Failing to widen the interval before solving for 2x or (x − 30°), then narrowing it again.
- Mixing degrees and radians within the same question.
