=== intro ===
Modulus, polynomial division and partial fractions. Dry on its own, but almost everything later in Paper 3 depends on being able to split an awkward fraction into manageable pieces.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the meaning of |x| and solve equations and inequalities involving the modulus
- Divide a polynomial by a polynomial and identify the quotient and remainder
- Use the factor theorem and the remainder theorem
- Recall and use the expansions of expressions in partial fractions, including repeated and quadratic factors

=== mistakes ===
- Solving a modulus equation by squaring and not checking the answers back.
- Choosing the wrong partial fraction form for a repeated factor — it needs both (x + a) and (x + a)².
- Forgetting that an improper fraction must be divided out before splitting into partial fractions.
- Substituting x = a rather than x = −a when testing the factor (x + a).
