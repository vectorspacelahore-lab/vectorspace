=== intro ===
Extending the number system so every polynomial has roots. The Argand diagram turns algebra into geometry, and multiplication becomes rotation.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the idea of a complex number and the terms real part, imaginary part, modulus, argument and conjugate
- Carry out operations of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of complex numbers
- Use the result that complex roots of real polynomials occur in conjugate pairs
- Represent complex numbers on an Argand diagram and use modulus-argument form
- Illustrate simple equations and inequalities involving complex numbers as loci

=== mistakes ===
- Getting the argument in the wrong quadrant. Always sketch the point first.
- Forgetting to multiply by the conjugate when dividing complex numbers.
- Working in degrees when the argument should be in radians.
- Drawing the locus |z − a| = r centred at −a rather than a.
