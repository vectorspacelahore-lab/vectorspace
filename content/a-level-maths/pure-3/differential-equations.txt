=== intro ===
An equation relating a quantity to its own rate of change. Separating the variables turns it into two integrals, and the initial condition pins down the constant.


=== syllabus ===
- Formulate a simple statement involving a rate of change as a differential equation
- Find by integration a general form of solution for a first order equation with separable variables
- Use an initial condition to find a particular solution
- Interpret the solution of a differential equation in context

=== mistakes ===
- Separating variables incorrectly, leaving an x with the dy.
- Forgetting the constant of integration, or applying the initial condition before integrating.
- Mishandling the constant when exponentiating — e^(x + c) becomes Ae^x, not e^x + A.
- Not answering the contextual question at the end, such as the long-term behaviour.
