=== intro ===
The full toolkit: products, quotients, chains, implicit and parametric differentiation. Most Paper 3 questions combine two of these in one expression.


=== syllabus ===
- Differentiate eˣ, ln x, sin x, cos x, tan x, tan⁻¹x and compositions of these
- Differentiate products and quotients
- Find and use the first derivative of a function defined implicitly or parametrically
- Locate stationary points and determine their nature

=== mistakes ===
- Applying the quotient rule with the numerator and denominator terms the wrong way round.
- Forgetting the chain rule inside a product or quotient.
- In implicit differentiation, differentiating a y-term without attaching dy/dx.
- In parametric work, dividing the derivatives the wrong way round: dy/dx is (dy/dt) ÷ (dx/dt).
