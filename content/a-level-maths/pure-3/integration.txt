=== intro ===
Standard forms, substitution, parts and partial fractions. The skill is recognising which method a given integral wants before starting.


=== syllabus ===
- Integrate eᵃˣ⁺ᵇ, 1/(ax + b), sin(ax + b), cos(ax + b) and sec²(ax + b)
- Integrate rational functions by means of decomposition into partial fractions
- Recognise and integrate expressions of the form f′(x)/f(x)
- Integrate by parts and by substitution
- Use definite integration to find areas and volumes of revolution

=== mistakes ===
- Choosing u and dv the wrong way round in integration by parts, making the integral harder.
- Forgetting to change the limits when using a substitution in a definite integral.
- Missing that an integral is of the form f′(x)/f(x) and attempting something much longer.
- Losing the modulus signs inside logarithms.
