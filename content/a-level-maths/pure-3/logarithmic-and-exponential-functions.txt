=== intro ===
Logarithms free the unknown from the exponent, and the natural exponential is the function that is its own derivative. Both feed directly into calculus and differential equations later.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the relationship between logarithms and indices and use the laws of logarithms
- Understand the definition and properties of eˣ and ln x, including their graphs
- Solve equations of the form aˣ = b
- Use logarithms to transform a relationship to linear form and find the constants

=== mistakes ===
- Expanding log(a + b) as log a + log b.
- Taking logs of one term rather than the whole side of an equation.
- Accepting a solution that requires the log of a negative number.
- Confusing the gradient and intercept with the constants they represent after a log transformation.
