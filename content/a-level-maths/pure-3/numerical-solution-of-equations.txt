=== intro ===
When an equation can't be solved exactly, you locate a root by sign change and then close in on it iteratively. The syllabus cares as much about justifying the root as finding it.


=== syllabus ===
- Locate approximately a root of an equation by graphical means or by a sign change
- Understand the idea of and use the notation for an iterative sequence xₙ₊₁ = F(xₙ)
- Use a given iteration to find a root to a specified degree of accuracy
- Understand how the behaviour of the sequence relates to convergence

=== mistakes ===
- Showing a sign change without stating that the function is continuous over the interval.
- Rounding at each step of an iteration instead of carrying full accuracy through.
- Stopping the iteration before consecutive values agree to the required accuracy.
- Leaving the calculator in degrees when the equation involves trigonometric functions.
