=== intro ===
The compound and double angle formulas, and the R-formula for combining a sine and a cosine into one wave. This is where trigonometry stops being about triangles and becomes an algebraic tool.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the expansions of sin(A ± B), cos(A ± B) and tan(A ± B)
- Use the double angle formulas and the identities for sec²θ and cosec²θ
- Express a sin θ + b cos θ in the forms R sin(θ ± α) and R cos(θ ± α)
- Use these to solve equations and find maximum and minimum values

=== mistakes ===
- Sign errors in the compound angle formulas, especially cos(A + B) where the sign flips.
- Choosing the wrong double angle form for cos 2θ — pick the one that matches what else is in the equation.
- Getting α in the wrong quadrant when finding R sin(θ + α).
- Forgetting that the maximum of R sin(θ + α) is R, not R + α.
