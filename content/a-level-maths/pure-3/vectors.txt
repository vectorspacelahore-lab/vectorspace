=== intro ===
Lines and planes in three dimensions. Once a line is written as a point plus a multiple of a direction, questions about angles, intersections and distances become routine.


=== syllabus ===
- Use standard notation for vectors and carry out addition, subtraction and multiplication by a scalar
- Use unit vectors, displacement vectors and position vectors
- Calculate the scalar product and use it to find angles and to test perpendicularity
- Understand the vector equation of a line and determine whether two lines are parallel, intersect or are skew
- Find the point of intersection of two lines when it exists

=== mistakes ===
- Concluding two lines intersect after solving only two of the three component equations. Always check the third.
- Taking the angle between two lines without using the magnitudes in the scalar product formula.
- Confusing the position vector of a point with the direction vector of the line.
- Giving an obtuse angle where the acute one was asked for — take the modulus of the scalar product.
