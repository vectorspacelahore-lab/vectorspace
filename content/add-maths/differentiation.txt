=== intro ===
The derivative measures how fast something changes. Once you can find one, you can find gradients, tangents, maxima, minima and rates of change — which is most of the applied content in the paper.


=== syllabus ===
- Differentiate xⁿ, eˣ, ln x, sin x, cos x and tan x
- Use the chain, product and quotient rules
- Find the equations of tangents and normals to a curve
- Find and classify stationary points using the first or second derivative
- Apply differentiation to connected rates of change and small increments

=== mistakes ===
- Using the product rule where the chain rule is needed, or applying both at once without brackets.
- Finding stationary points but not classifying them, when the question asks for a maximum or minimum specifically.
- Using the gradient of the tangent for the normal. The normal's gradient is the negative reciprocal.
- In rates-of-change problems, setting up the chain of derivatives in the wrong order.
