=== intro ===
One question decides everything in this topic: does the order matter? If it does you want a permutation, if it doesn't you want a combination, and almost every lost mark traces back to answering that question wrongly.


=== syllabus ===
- Recognise and distinguish between permutations and combinations
- Evaluate nPr and nCr and use them in problems
- Solve problems about arrangements of objects in a line, including repetition and restriction
- Solve problems about selections from a set

=== mistakes ===
- Using a permutation for a selection where order is irrelevant, such as choosing a committee.
- Handling 'at least' conditions by adding cases incorrectly — often it's quicker to count the complement.
- Forgetting to divide by the factorials of repeated letters when arranging a word with repeats.
- Treating a restriction like 'two people must sit together' as separate when they should be blocked as one unit first.
