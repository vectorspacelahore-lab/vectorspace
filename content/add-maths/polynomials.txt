=== intro ===
The remainder and factor theorems turn division into substitution. If you can find one root of a cubic you can factor it out and reduce the problem to a quadratic, which you already know how to solve completely.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the factor theorem and the remainder theorem
- Divide a polynomial by a linear or quadratic divisor
- Fully factorise a cubic polynomial given one factor, or found by trial
- Solve cubic equations and sketch the resulting curves
- Use the identity for the sum and difference of two cubes

=== mistakes ===
- Substituting x = a instead of x = −a when testing the factor (x + a).
- Stopping after finding one root. A cubic has three roots, and the quadratic factor usually gives two more.
- Making sign errors in the long-division layout — the most common single source of lost marks in this topic.
- Forgetting the remainder theorem gives the remainder, not the quotient, when the question asks for the full division.
