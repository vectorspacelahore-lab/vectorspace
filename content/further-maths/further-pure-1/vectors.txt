=== intro ===
Lines and planes in three dimensions, and the two products that make the geometry tractable: the scalar product for angles and the vector product for perpendiculars.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the equation of a line in vector and Cartesian form
- Use the equation of a plane in vector, scalar product and Cartesian form
- Calculate the vector product and use it to find areas and normals
- Find the angle between two lines, a line and a plane, or two planes
- Find the distance from a point to a line or a plane, and between two skew lines

=== mistakes ===
- Confusing the scalar and vector products, or their geometric meanings.
- Concluding two lines intersect after checking only two of the three components.
- Using the angle between a line and the normal to a plane as the angle with the plane itself.
- Forgetting the modulus when finding the distance between two skew lines — the formula gives a signed value, and distance is never negative.
