=== intro ===
Extending differentiation to inverse trigonometric and hyperbolic functions, implicit relations and parametric curves, plus Maclaurin series.


=== syllabus ===
- Differentiate inverse trigonometric and inverse hyperbolic functions
- Use implicit and parametric differentiation, including second derivatives
- Find and use Maclaurin series for standard functions
- Determine the nature of stationary points using higher derivatives

=== mistakes ===
- Finding the second derivative parametrically by differentiating dy/dx with respect to t and stopping — it must then be divided by dx/dt.
- Forgetting the chain rule factor when differentiating an inverse function of something other than x.
- Truncating a Maclaurin series before the number of terms the question asked for.
- Differentiating a y-term implicitly without attaching dy/dx.
