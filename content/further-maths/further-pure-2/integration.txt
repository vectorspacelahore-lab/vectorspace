=== intro ===
Reduction formulas, arc length, surface area and improper integrals — the techniques for integrals that resist the standard methods.


=== syllabus ===
- Derive and use reduction formulas for the evaluation of definite integrals
- Use integration to find arc lengths and surface areas of revolution
- Evaluate improper integrals and recognise when they diverge
- Use hyperbolic and trigonometric substitutions

=== mistakes ===
- Deriving a reduction formula but not stating the base case needed to use it.
- Forgetting to change the limits after a substitution in a definite integral.
- Treating an improper integral as ordinary without taking the limit, and so missing a divergence.
- Losing the square root or the factor of 2π in arc length and surface area formulas.
