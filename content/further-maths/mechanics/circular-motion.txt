=== intro ===
Motion in a circle at constant speed still has acceleration, because the direction keeps changing. That acceleration points at the centre and must be caused by a real force.


=== syllabus ===
- Understand the concept of angular speed and use v = rω
- Use the expressions for acceleration in a circle: v²/r and rω²
- Solve problems involving motion in a horizontal circle
- Solve problems involving motion in a vertical circle, including conditions for maintaining contact

=== mistakes ===
- Treating the centripetal force as an extra force in the diagram rather than the resultant of the real ones.
- Using v²/r with ω instead of v, or the reverse.
- In vertical circles, forgetting that speed changes and energy must be conserved.
- Missing the condition for a particle to leave the surface, where the normal reaction reaches zero.
