=== intro ===
Elastic strings and springs exert a force proportional to their extension. Because that force varies, energy methods are usually easier than force methods.


=== syllabus ===
- Use Hooke's law in the form T = λx/l
- Understand and use the concept of elastic potential energy
- Solve problems involving elastic strings and springs, including equilibrium and motion
- Use conservation of energy in problems involving elastic elements

=== mistakes ===
- Using the natural length instead of the extension in Hooke's law.
- Forgetting that a string can only pull, never push, so it goes slack rather than compressing.
- Omitting elastic potential energy from a conservation of energy statement.
- Confusing the modulus of elasticity with the stiffness constant.
