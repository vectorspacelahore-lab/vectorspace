=== intro ===
When force depends on position or velocity, acceleration is no longer constant and calculus is required. Choosing the right form of acceleration is the whole battle.


=== syllabus ===
- Use Newton's second law with acceleration expressed as dv/dt or v dv/dx
- Solve problems where the force depends on time, displacement or velocity
- Set up and solve the resulting differential equation
- Interpret the solution, including terminal velocity

=== mistakes ===
- Using dv/dt when the force depends on displacement, where v dv/dx is the useful form.
- Losing the constant of integration and with it the initial condition.
- Separating variables incorrectly and leaving mixed terms on one side.
- Forgetting that terminal velocity occurs where acceleration, not velocity, is zero.
