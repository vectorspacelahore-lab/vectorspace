description: CIE A Level Further Mathematics 9231 past papers — yearly question papers, mark schemes and fully solved papers, plus topical past papers grouped by syllabus topic.


=== intro ===
Two ways to use these. **Topical** papers group every past question on a single topic together — the right tool while you're still learning something. **Yearly** papers are whole exams under timed conditions, which is what you want in the last few weeks.

Work topically first, then yearly. Doing it the other way round usually means sitting a full paper before you're ready for one, which teaches you very little except how to feel bad.
