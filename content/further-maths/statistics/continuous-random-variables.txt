=== intro ===
A variable that can take any value in a range, described by a density function. Probability becomes area, and sums become integrals.


=== syllabus ===
- Use a probability density function to find probabilities, mean and variance
- Find and use the cumulative distribution function
- Find the median, quartiles and other percentiles of a continuous distribution
- Find the distribution of a function of a continuous random variable

=== mistakes ===
- Integrating over the wrong interval, or over regions where the density is zero.
- Forgetting that the total area under the density function must be 1.
- Using E(X²) as the variance without subtracting the square of the mean.
- Confusing the density function with the cumulative distribution function when finding the median.
