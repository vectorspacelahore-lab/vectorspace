=== intro ===
Using a sample to say something about a population, and being honest about the uncertainty. The t-distribution handles small samples where the normal would be over-confident.


=== syllabus ===
- Calculate unbiased estimates of the population mean and variance
- Formulate hypotheses and carry out tests for a population mean using a normal distribution
- Use the t-distribution for small samples from a normal population with unknown variance
- Determine and interpret confidence intervals for a population mean and for a difference of means

=== mistakes ===
- Using the normal distribution where the sample is small and the variance unknown.
- Dividing by n rather than n − 1 when estimating the population variance.
- Reading the t-table with the wrong number of degrees of freedom.
- Stating a conclusion without reference to the context of the question.
