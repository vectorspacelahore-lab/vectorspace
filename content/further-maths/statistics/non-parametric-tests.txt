=== intro ===
Tests that make no assumption about the shape of the underlying distribution. Useful precisely when the data is not normal.


=== syllabus ===
- Use the sign test for a population median
- Use the Wilcoxon signed-rank test for a population median and for paired samples
- Use the Wilcoxon rank-sum test for two independent samples
- Determine critical values and state conclusions in context

=== mistakes ===
- Keeping a zero difference in the sample. Any value equal to the hypothesised median is discarded, and n must be reduced to match before you look up the critical value.
- Assigning ranks to the raw values rather than to the absolute differences in a signed-rank test.
- Forgetting to take the smaller of T+ and T− as the test statistic.
- Comparing the test statistic to the critical value in the wrong direction — for these tests you reject the null hypothesis when the statistic is small, not large.
- Stating the conclusion as a bare inequality. The marks are for the sentence about the situation in the question.
