Four courses, all starting **14 September**, all preparing for the **May/June 2027** examinations.

Classes are live on Zoom, running for seven months and finishing with a full past-paper session before the examinations. Every session is recorded, and recordings are provided for any class a student misses — illness, emergencies, other commitments — so that nobody falls behind.

## How the courses are taught

**Curiosity, then exploration, then understanding.**

We don't start with a method. We start with a problem worth being curious about, and we go looking. We try the obvious things, watch them get ugly, and only then meet the technique that rescues us — so it arrives as an answer to a question you were already asking, rather than a rule to memorise.

That order matters more than anything else I do. A method you were handed is something you revise. A method you saw the need for is something you understand, and understanding is what survives an exam question phrased in a way you've never seen. Once you know *why* something works, you don't forget it.

Groups stay small enough that questions actually get answered.
